CatClean Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

In one line: CatClean collects no data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no crash reporting.

Where your data is processed

Entirely on your device. Library scanning, duplicate and similar photo detection, blur detection, video compression, and file hashing all run locally. Scan results are stored inside the app's own sandbox.

Network

The app makes no network requests. There is no server, no third-party SDK, no analytics, and no advertising.

Why the app asks for these permissions

PermissionWhat it is for
Photo library (read)Finding duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, screen recordings, large videos, and blurry photos worth cleaning up
Photo library (write)Deleting the photos and videos you select, and only after you confirm
Folder accessOnly the folders you explicitly grant, used to find duplicate files, rank them by size, and move them to the trash

Everything read through these permissions stays on your device. None of it leaves.

What happens when data is deleted

  • Deleting from your library goes through Apple's Photos framework and lands in Recently Deleted first, where it can be recovered.
  • Deleting files moves them into CatClean's own trash, where you can restore them in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all local data with it: scan results, trash, and settings.

Third parties

None. The app bundles no third-party SDK and shares data with nobody — there is no data leaving the device to share.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change with it.

Contact

For questions about this policy or how the app handles data, leave a note on the project issue tracker.
