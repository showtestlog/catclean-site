On-device photo and file cleanup · iOS

Why is there no "Clean Everything" button?

Because the suggestions include screenshots, screen recordings, and blurry photos — whole categories, in the hundreds. One button that deletes all of them takes the decision away from you. CatClean finds things and puts them in order; which ones to delete is your call, made one category at a time.

The results don't match what I remember having

Scanning uses asset metadata plus pixel analysis, and a few categories only appear when the files themselves carry a marker written by the system:

  • Screenshots and screen recordings must be produced by the system's own capture. An image imported from elsewhere is not put in this category, even if its filename says "screenshot".
  • Bursts and Live Photos only show up if your library actually contains some.

Similar photos look wrong in the Simulator

The Simulator has no Metal inference context, so Vision cannot build feature prints and the app falls back to a grayscale signature. On a real device it uses Vision, which is more accurate.

Where did deleted photos go?

Deletions from your library go through iOS's own Recently Deleted and can be recovered. Deleted files go to CatClean's trash, where you can restore them from Files › Trash.

Does video slimming lose quality?

It re-encodes at the same resolution and lowers the bitrate, which is invisible in everyday viewing. The original bitstream cannot be recovered from the compressed version, though, which is why the app asks whether to delete the original or keep both.

Why does it need photo access at all?

Without it the app can't read any assets, so there is nothing to scan. Nothing is uploaded and no network requests are made — see the privacy policy.

Still stuck

Leave a note on the project issue tracker with your device model, iOS version, and the steps to reproduce. A review comment on the App Store works too.
